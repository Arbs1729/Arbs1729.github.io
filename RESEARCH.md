# Reference research — 12 September 2026

Research set: 24 personal sites/creator portfolios, plus two adjacent interaction references. Direct homepages were read with the web reader; Henry, Poolsuite and Sean also inspected in the browser. The table distinguishes extraction-based observations from visual inspection. Trade-offs are judgments about applying a pattern to this portfolio, not claims of defects in the originals. No assets or layouts copied.

| Reference | Useful pattern / what works | Transfer risk / what to avoid |

Unavailable/insufficient: Robby Leonardi interactive-resume route and Adam Chapman failed reader access; Oshin failed reader access; Samsy returned a title without content. These are not counted as reviewed references or used to justify unseen visual details.

## Synthesis

Adopt physical controls, localised play, visible sections, compact work summaries, separate depth, and visually coherent personal collections. Reject navigation puzzles, mandatory loaders, autoplay sound, fake live market data and unnecessary window management. The chosen flight deck mixes an instrument panel with a normal editorial scroll; its original orbital object and panel artwork tie together product systems, finance and racing/anime references.
